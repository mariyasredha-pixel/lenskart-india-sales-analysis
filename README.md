# 👓 Lenskart India Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing Lenskart India sales performance to explore sales distribution, compare online and offline channels, and identify trends across product categories, cities, payment modes, and store types.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

![Lenskart India Sales Analysis Dashboard](lenskart-dashboard.png)

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Net Sales:** ₹784M
- **Average Unit Price:** ₹434M (as displayed in the dashboard)
- **Average Discount:** ₹1M (as displayed in the dashboard)
- **Total Quantity:** 300K (as displayed in the dashboard)

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Insights

- **Sales by Payment Mode:** Compares net sales across Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, and UPI.
- **Yearly Sales Trend:** Visualizes sales trends from 2022 to 2025.
- **Online vs Offline Sales:** Compares sales contributions from online and offline channels.
- **Sales by Product Category:** Examines sales across Computer Glasses, Contact Lenses, Eyeglasses, and Sunglasses.
- **City-Wise Sales Analysis:** Compares net sales across cities, including Cuttack, Hyderabad, Indore, Kochi, and Trivandrum.
- **Customer Gender Analysis:** Visualizes sales distribution by Female, Male, and Other customer groups.
- **Sales by Store Type:** Compares sales across Online Only, High Street Store, Standalone Store, and Mall Store.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance using key metrics.
- Compare online and offline sales channels.
- Identify sales distribution across product categories and payment methods.
- Explore yearly sales trends and city-wise performance.
- Understand customer gender distribution and store-type contributions.
- Present business data through interactive visualizations.
